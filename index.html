Applied AI

  • Week 1 – Introduction to artificial intelligence

    Definitions of artificial intelligence and the four approaches to it, its relationship to machine learning, its history, sub-fields and current state of the art, and its risks and benefits.

  • Week 2 – Uninformed and informed search

    Search problems, search trees and the frontier, complexity and Big-O notation, uninformed strategies from breadth-first to iterative deepening search, graph search, and informed search with greedy best-first search and A*.

  • Week 3 – Adversarial search and games

    Formal definition of two-player games, minimax search, alpha–beta pruning, heuristic cut-off with evaluation functions, expectiminimax for games of chance, and approaches to games of imperfect information.

  • Week 4 – Intelligent agents and environments

    Agents, rationality and performance measures, PEAS task descriptions and environment properties, the basic agent architectures, and the formulation of problems for goal-based agents.

  • Week 5 – Knowledge representation and logical reasoning

    Knowledge-based agents and logical representation: propositional logic, entailment, model checking, forward and backward chaining, resolution, first-order logic and its inference, and logic programming in Prolog.
